# Cardio Slot visual direction

Approved 17 September 2026. The former flat console/two-column configurator is historical.

## Cabinet

One physical enclosure: cream enamel with darker sidewalls, rolled chrome perimeter, red CARDIO SLOT marquee, recessed dark glass drums, and a right-hand lever rail with bracket, pivot, rod and red knob. The three drums reveal one coordinated workout type: a mirrored line-art mark frames Endurance, Hills, or Speed. Side marks are decorative reinforcement and never separate workout settings. Integrated controls label the 15, 30, and 60 minute choices “Duration” in English and give each bookend a wide hardware rocker. In the two-row deck, the duration group’s left edge aligns with the warm-up rocker and its right edge with the cool-down rocker; both derive their alignment from the same rocker width and spacing. A recessed printer well feeds a short leading receipt through a slit behind a foreground lip.

The machine uses the Track finish in cream, red, and chrome; legacy saved finish values normalize to Track. Material tokens remain separate from workout semantics. Typography follows shared roles: IBM Plex Mono for English UI, small CARDIO SLOT wordmarks, and numeric data in both languages; the bundled Noto Sans TC for Chinese UI; Barlow Condensed 800 only for the large cabinet marquee. Ticket titles and section numbers use these roles rather than a separate serif. Body is 17px regular, controls 16px medium, section headings 19px semibold, and metadata 12px medium; body line height is 1.6 in both languages. Receipt instructions are at least 16px; interactive targets at least 48×48px.

The cabinet carries no introductory tagline or visual-style selector. “Pull to start” and the ready-state knob halo establish the primary action on initial load and after Pull again; an existing ticket keeps the invitation inactive. Installation, help and offline status are secondary. Total includes enabled warm-up, cooldown and recoveries.

The initial machine entry uses a stationary lever with a soft repeating knob halo and a small downward chevron beneath the localized prompt. The first-pull attention state gates both decorations; pulling or retaining a ticket removes the invitation. Reduced motion keeps the halo and chevron static. A compact utility header places the static CARDIO SLOT wordmark at left and the conditional Install action plus a 48px globe language button at right; there is no global footer. The language picker opens English / 繁體中文 options and closes on selection, Escape, outside click, or focus leaving it. The globe remains in the global header before and after a session; the ticket, countdown, running view, and end confirmation keep the chosen language without a local control. Install never appears during an active session. English and Traditional Chinese are bundled and work offline. A saved manual choice overrides browser detection; zh-TW, zh-Hant, zh-HK, zh-MO and zh-CN select Traditional Chinese. Changing language translates existing tickets, active cues, accessibility announcements, metadata and result images without regenerating or restarting the workout. CARDIO SLOT remains English, functional labels use natural Taiwan fitness language, and Chinese uses a bundled, subsetted Noto Sans TC variable font with local CJK fallback. Chinese body text uses regular weight and 1.6 line height; semibold headings keep natural character spacing and strict Chinese punctuation breaking. Numeric running readouts use IBM Plex Mono with tabular numerals and a bounded size for five-character clocks. The PNG explicitly loads the same Chinese font and retains a bounded fallback. Font provenance, regeneration and evidence are recorded in `docs/plans/traditional-chinese-typography-fix-plan.md`.

## Responsive behavior

Portrait reserves a lever rail and keeps the complete cabinet discoverable at 390×844. The 360px layout preserves text and control sizing. Below 700px, the central drum has extra width for English names; numeric animation geometry remains shared. Desktop/tablet views at least 800px tall grow the marquee and reel rows with bounded viewport sizing; reel position and animation retain the same shared geometry variables. Short landscape (`orientation: landscape` and `max-height: 540px`) reflows to a lower/wider cabinet: marquee and drums span the width, duration and bookends share the deck, finishes and outlet occupy its lower row. Exceptionally short configuration views scroll vertically.

Receipt uses a native, viewport-limited modal around one naturally tall, warm-white paper slip. Its selected drawn-slip direction uses a narrow vertical wordmark rail, an asymmetric workout-type and duration heading, red section numbers, fine rules, aligned data columns, and quiet duration-proportional effort strips. The whole sheet scrolls so its heading, itinerary, and actions move together. Every block, including Warm-up, Recovery, and Cool-down, prints each interval's exact time, textual effort, and incline in chronological order. No workout details are hidden behind disclosures or represented only by color. Main blocks retain sequential numbered headings matching their block labels (01, 02, 03), excluding supporting phases from the count, and full tables, with each effort strip between the phase heading and table. Warm-up, Recovery, and Cool-down use compact margin-note rows showing exact time, effort and incline, without repeated table headings or strips. A thin margin rule connects numbered main blocks and quiet supporting-phase dots. Every interval retains its accessible list and incline label. A compact static effort key uses shared, content-sized label columns and concise bilingual descriptions; descriptions align to the paper’s right edge and rows fit at 320px while allowing wrapping under text enlargement. The margin itinerary ends at the final phase. The guide uses the whole paper width as a closing color key: a small muted heading, no row rules, narrow effort markers reusing the exact strip colors, dark effort labels, and right-aligned secondary-size explanations. Color supplements the text rather than replacing it. The safety footnote is omitted in both languages. Ticket footer actions reuse the result page’s primary and secondary button styling, including rounded corners and outlined secondary actions. Close preserves the preview, and View ticket reopens it. Paper and modal use the same leading layout; a non-modal handoff measures the source in viewport coordinates and scales/reveals the real receipt before the dialog opens and takes focus. Resize, unavailable animation, and reduced motion settle directly into the usable dialog.

Result screens place a localized Workout complete / Session summary heading and a short save/share prompt above the exact 1080×1080 PNG, without a visible duplicate data summary. Completed sessions show a brief decorative confetti burst that never intercepts pointer input or enters the exported image; confetti remains enabled even when system reduced motion is set, as requested. The burst runs once per mounted completed result and can replay when that result is reopened. The selected dark profile card uses a small Cardio Slot wordmark/date, a neutral workout title and completion state, then a lime stepped timeline. Hills plots prescribed incline; Endurance and Speed plot categorical prescribed effort, never measured pace or heart rate. Only elapsed interval portions appear, with a neutral empty state at zero time. Two equal metrics show elapsed time and highest prescribed incline reached; zero-time incline is unknown. Block counts are omitted from both the image and its accessible equivalent. Four effort durations sit in a two-column footer. English labels, branding and numeric values use IBM Plex Mono; Traditional Chinese labels use the bundled Noto Sans TC. The image has an equivalent semantic HTML description, including elapsed intervals, for assistive technology; if rendering fails, that summary becomes visible. Loading reserves the square preview’s aspect ratio. Actions are Download PNG and Pull again, on both mobile and desktop. Download filenames include the template type, canonical recorded UTC session timestamp down to milliseconds, and completed/ended status; repeated downloads of one result intentionally retain the same base filename. Native-canvas artifacts verify the source exporter; actual browser/device rendering remains a separate acceptance check.


Running has stable semantic backgrounds until the exact interval boundary. A duration-proportional workout map shows every canonical phase and interval, without visible phase labels or an active-block outline, retaining current/future effort colors, a continuously muted neutral overlay over the elapsed portion (including the partial current interval), and a continuously interpolated playhead. The playhead follows wall-clock time; interval boundaries still drive cue and background changes. Its accessible value names elapsed time, phase, effort, and time left in the interval. Elapsed and remaining labels precede their tabular times on the same line. The current cue shows effort, countdown, a running corgi, and incline without a block title, instruction line, or top/bottom borders; its ruled incline readout sits beneath the corgi with extra separation on tall screens. Tall views give the timer, corgi, and incline separate breathing room while compact layouts keep all controls reachable. Easy retains its gentle running cadence; Strong and Max use distinctly faster cadence, increasingly visible speed lines, focused/open eyes, and a longer panting tongue. Walk uses a separate four-footfall cycle with a level body and upright legs, rather than slowing the airborne gallop. Walk/run poses cross-fade over 650ms while running pace eases without restarting its animation. Hidden gait players pause after the blend. Decorative motion is suppressed for reduced motion. Phase and instruction details remain in screen-reader announcements. A bounded next-interval card aligns its label, effort, duration, and incline and uses the upcoming interval’s semantic background and foreground colors, independently of the current screen. It changes to “NEXT IN” and strengthens its border only for the final five seconds without reflow or changing the upcoming palette. The final FINISH card retains the neutral inset surface. The destructive action is the quieter “■ End session.” Portrait keeps the map, current cue, next dock, and End in that order; narrow screens place next-effort details on two lines to retain readable text. Short landscape retains the 65/35 split, with the current cue dominant and next/End/options in a compact secondary rail. Grid tracks and cue containers allow shrinking rather than imposing intrinsic text widths. At 320px portrait, compact caption/time roles keep both header groups inside the viewport; short portrait spacing retains the complete End button at 320×640. The corgi frame scales to its available width with a relative baseline offset. Safe-area insets and dynamic viewport units apply. No orientation restriction in the PWA manifest. Rotation never alters workout or timer state.

## Mechanical timing

Lever pull 0–220ms and returns through 520ms. The drums accelerate for roughly 350ms, run at unreadable speed, then settle at 3200/3600/4000ms. Leading paper feeds for 650ms, pauses for 200ms, and completes its automatic handoff at 5500ms. Forward lever pivot stays inside the reserved rail. State-driven fallbacks reach a usable ticket if motion cannot complete, and request IDs guard stale completions. Reduced motion skips the spatial sequence and reveals the dialog immediately.

## Acceptance

Inspect portrait 320×640,360×800,390×844,430×932; desktop 1440×900; landscape 844×390,932×430,844×320, all finishes and reduced motion. Timed frames/video are required for mechanics. Physical iPhone Safari and Android Chrome, browser and installed modes, plus a real 15-minute treadmill session remain the final human acceptance gate.

Wake-lock success and request states are silent. Denied or unsupported states display “Keep your screen on — automatic display lock is unavailable.” Pull another returns to the cabinet without the old ticket affordance, then runs the complete mechanical sequence and rejects an exact visible repeat of the completed workout.
